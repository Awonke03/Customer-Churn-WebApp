# South African dataset selection

Preferred source: Zindi June Study Jam Series: Bank Transaction Volume Forecasting Challenge (June 10–30, 2026). https://zindi.world/competitions/june-study-jam-series-transaction-volume-forecasting-challenge/data
The competition page lists CC BY-SA 4.0 and does not display the deletion clause of the earlier prize competition. The files require authenticated access and acceptance of terms. Confirm that the June release terms govern the downloaded files and downstream artifacts before publishing. Do not use copies from the earlier restricted competition. No Nedbank records have been acquired or trained here.

The June release describes 11,944 customers, 18 million dated transactions, 372,000 monthly financial records, and a separate demographic table. For a future local model, construct historical snapshots with 30- and 90-day inactivity targets, actual product-revenue forecasts from monthly financial records, and customer-disjoint temporal validation. Exclude future information and sensitive demographics from operational decisions; separately audit subgroups where lawful. Use native ZAR. Do not conflate turnover with revenue.

South African Financial Diaries (https://doi.org/10.25828/gegg-sk25) provides household financial flows in 2003–2004; its 160-household sample is not a substitute for contemporary bank customer retention labels. Global Findex provides population context rather than customer-level longitudinal labels.

Current production model remains COFINFAD until a local dataset is acquired and validated.
